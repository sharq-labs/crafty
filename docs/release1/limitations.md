# Release 1 limitations

Scientific Discovery Platform — Release 1 is one bounded typed product
candidate. The following are explicitly not provided or claimed:

- no general physical validation;
- no production digital-twin registry;
- no PostgreSQL;
- no S3;
- no Redis;
- no distributed execution;
- no cloud runtime;
- no CFD;
- no FEA;
- no general multi-fidelity execution;
- no real/general UQ;
- no BoTorch;
- no Bayesian optimization;
- no autonomous repeated discovery;
- no Generation 2+;
- no convergence claim for a discovery process;
- no hypothesis intelligence;
- no general scientific intelligence;
- no universal optimization;
- no LLM/AI orchestration;
- no UI or web service;
- no medical or clinical validation;
- no safety or certification claim.

The D4/D5/D6/D7 closed-loop reference is a synthetic analytic fixture. Its
typed `ScientificResult` objects, attribution, memory, decision, execution,
and replay are real software behavior, but that behavior is not
physical-world validation or real-world discovery.

The proven Electrical DC, Thermal conduction1d, Kinetics CSTR, rotor-hover,
and multirotor paths retain their domain-local assumptions and validation
envelopes. Their presence does not create a general physics engine. Every
additional domain requires independent numerical/domain verification and
appropriate real-world validation before claims can be made for that domain.

Deterministic serialization and hashing detect equality or change under the
implemented conventions. They do not provide a digital signature,
cryptographic authenticity, independent replication, physical validation, or
safety certification.
